A small group of friends went to the river early in the morning to watch birds. Rain had fallen during the night, so the path was wet and the air smelled of grass and soil. We stopped near a wooden bridge and opened our notebooks. Two ducks crossed the water, and a tiny gray bird appeared among the reeds. None of us knew its name, so we planned to look it up later. On the way home the sun came out from behind the clouds. We wrote down our observations and agreed to return next month to see what had changed.
