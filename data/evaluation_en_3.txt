Our neighborhood opened a small library with a room for meetings. Visitors can bring a book, leave a note with a recommendation, or suggest a topic for discussion. On the first day we talked about travel and looked at old photographs of the town. Many familiar streets had once looked completely different. After the meeting the librarian suggested an evening of board games. We helped move the tables, made a list of participants, and decided to invite more neighbors so that everyone could enjoy the new space.
